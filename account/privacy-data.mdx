MedifyAI is built with privacy at its core. Your health data is yours — MedifyAI does not sell it, and you can export or delete it at any time. This page explains your data rights and how to exercise them.

## How your data is used

MedifyAI uses your personal and health data strictly to:

- **Power AI health recommendations** — your data is used to generate relevant, personalized suggestions within your account.
- **Display your health records** — documents, lab results, and history are stored and surfaced for your own use.
- **Fulfill your care requests** — such as scheduling appointments or submitting prescription refill requests.

Your data is **never used for advertising** and is **never sold to third parties**. Period.

## HIPAA compliance

MedifyAI is a HIPAA-covered entity. All Protected Health Information (PHI) that you store or transmit through MedifyAI is safeguarded by the following controls:

- **Encryption at rest** — all PHI is encrypted using AES-256.
- **Encryption in transit** — all data transmitted to and from MedifyAI uses TLS 1.3.
- **Audit logging** — access to PHI is logged and retained for audit and compliance purposes.
- **Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)** — any third-party service that handles PHI on MedifyAI's behalf is bound by a signed BAA.

## Your rights

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Right to access your data">
    You can download a full export of your MedifyAI data at any time. Go to **Account Settings** → **Privacy** → **Export My Data** and submit a request. Your export file will be delivered to your registered email address within **48 hours**.

    The export includes your profile, health records, appointment history, AI conversation history, and medication logs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Right to correct your data">
    You can update or correct your personal information and health records directly from your **Profile** page or the **Health Records** section. If you need to correct data you cannot edit yourself, contact [support@medifyai.com](mailto:support@medifyai.com).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Right to delete your data">
    To request permanent deletion of your data, go to **Account Settings** → **Privacy** → **Delete My Data**. All PHI is purged from MedifyAI's systems within **30 days** of your request.

    You can also request early deletion by emailing [support@medifyai.com](mailto:support@medifyai.com).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Right to restrict processing">
    You can request that MedifyAI limit how it processes your data without deleting it. Contact [privacy@medifyai.com](mailto:privacy@medifyai.com) to submit a restriction request. We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="GDPR rights (EU residents)">
    If you are a resident of the European Union, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

    - **Right to data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
    - **Right to object** — object to certain types of processing, including profiling.
    - **Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")** — request deletion beyond standard account closure.

    To exercise your GDPR rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at [dpo@medifyai.com](mailto:dpo@medifyai.com).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data sharing with providers

MedifyAI only shares your health records with providers you **explicitly authorize**. No record is visible to a provider unless you have granted them access.

To manage or revoke provider access at any time:
1. Go to **Health Records** → **Sharing**.
2. Find the provider whose access you want to change.
3. Click **Revoke Access** to remove their permissions immediately.
