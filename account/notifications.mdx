MedifyAI sends notifications to keep you on top of appointments, medications, and health updates. You can customize exactly which notifications you receive and how you receive them.

## Accessing notification settings

Click your avatar → **Settings** → **Notifications**. From here you can toggle individual notification types on or off for each delivery channel.

## Notification types

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email">
    Control which events trigger an email to your registered address:

    - **Appointment confirmations** — sent immediately when you book or cancel an appointment
    - **Appointment reminders** — sent 24 hours and 1 hour before each appointment
    - **Prescription refill status** — updates when a refill request is approved, ready, or requires action
    - **Lab result ready** — notifies you when a new lab result is available in your Health Records
    - **Account security alerts** — login from a new device, password changes, and 2FA changes
    - **Weekly health summary** *(optional)* — a digest of your activity, upcoming appointments, and health tips
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Push">
    Push notifications are delivered to the MedifyAI mobile app or browser extension:

    - **Medication dose reminders** — alerts at your scheduled dose times
    - **Appointment reminders** — sent 24 hours and 1 hour before each appointment
    - **Message from provider** — notifies you when a provider sends you a message
    - **Symptom check follow-up** — reminds you to log symptoms or complete a follow-up check
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="SMS">
    SMS notifications require a verified phone number on your account.

    - **Appointment reminders** *(optional)* — a text reminder sent the day before your appointment

    To add or verify your phone number, go to **Account Settings** → **Contact Information**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Turning off all notifications

To temporarily stop all non-critical notifications, enable the **Pause All Notifications** toggle at the top of the Notifications settings page. You can also define a **quiet hours** window — for example, 10 PM – 7 AM — during which no notifications will be sent regardless of individual settings.

To set quiet hours:
1. Scroll to **Quiet Hours** in notification settings.
2. Toggle **Enable Quiet Hours** on.
3. Set your start and end time.

<Note>
  Security alerts — such as a login from a new device or a password change — **cannot be turned off**. They are required to keep your account safe.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Enable push notifications on the MedifyAI mobile app for the most timely medication and appointment reminders.
</Tip>
