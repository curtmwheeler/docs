Your MedifyAI account settings let you update contact information, change your password, enable two-factor authentication, and manage devices. This page covers all account management options.

## Updating your email or password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner of the app, then select **Account Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your email">
    Under **Email**, click **Change Email**. Enter your new email address and follow the verification link sent to it before the change takes effect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Under **Password**, click **Change Password**. Enter your current password, then type and confirm your new password. Click **Save** to apply the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds a second layer of protection beyond your password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Security settings">
    Navigate to **Account Settings** → **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable 2FA">
    Click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your method">
    Select either:
    - **Authenticator App** — works with Google Authenticator, Authy, or any TOTP-compatible app.
    - **SMS** — a one-time code is sent to your verified phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm setup">
    Follow the on-screen instructions to verify the method and complete setup. You'll be asked to enter a test code to confirm everything is working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  We strongly recommend enabling 2FA. Healthcare accounts are high-value targets — protect yours with a second factor.
</Tip>

<Note>
  2FA is **required** for accounts connected to provider portals and cannot be disabled on those accounts.
</Note>

## Connected devices

You can see every device currently logged in to your account under **Account Settings** → **Security** → **Active Sessions**. Each entry shows the device type, browser, approximate location, and last active time.

To log out a device remotely, click **Revoke** next to it. The session on that device ends immediately and the user will need to log in again.

## Linked accounts

MedifyAI supports single sign-on (SSO) with Google and Apple. If you signed up or linked an SSO provider, you can manage those connections from **Account Settings** → **Linked Accounts**.

- To **link** a new SSO provider, click **Connect** next to Google or Apple and complete the authorization flow.
- To **unlink** a provider, click **Disconnect**. Make sure you have a password set before unlinking, or you won't be able to log in.

## Closing your account

You can request account closure from **Account Settings** → **Danger Zone** → **Close Account**.

- Your account is deactivated immediately upon confirmation.
- Your data is retained for **30 days**, then permanently deleted in accordance with HIPAA data retention policies.
- To request early deletion before the 30-day window, contact [support@medifyai.com](mailto:support@medifyai.com).

<Warning>
  Account closure is permanent. Once your data is deleted after the 30-day period, it cannot be recovered.
</Warning>
