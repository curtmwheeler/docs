MedifyAI offers a free plan and premium subscription tiers to fit your healthcare needs. This page explains what each plan includes, how to upgrade, and how to manage your billing.

## Plans

## Upgrading your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Billing settings">
    Navigate to **Account Settings** → **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upgrade Plan">
    Click the **Upgrade Plan** button to open the plan selection screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Choose **Plus** or **Family** depending on your needs. A summary of what's included is shown for each option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Enter your credit or debit card information, or pay with **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your upgrade">
    Click **Confirm Upgrade**. Your new plan activates instantly — premium features are available right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing billing

All billing management is available from **Account Settings** → **Billing**:

- **View invoices** — a full history of past charges is listed under **Invoice History**. Click any invoice to download a PDF copy.
- **Update payment method** — click **Update Payment Method** to replace your card or switch to a different payment option.
- **Cancel auto-renewal** — click **Cancel Subscription** to stop your plan from renewing at the end of the current billing period. You keep access to premium features until the period ends.

## Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time — no hoops, no waiting. Your plan stays active through the end of the billing period you've already paid for.

**Refund policy:** MedifyAI does not issue refunds for partial months. If you believe a charge was made in error, contact [billing@medifyai.com](mailto:billing@medifyai.com) within 30 days of the charge and we'll review it promptly.

<Note>
  For enterprise or provider organization billing, see the Provider Onboarding section or contact [sales@medifyai.com](mailto:sales@medifyai.com).
</Note>
