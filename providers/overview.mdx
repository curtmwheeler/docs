## Getting started as a provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a provider account">
    Sign up at [app.medifyai.com/providers/signup](/providers/onboarding) to register your provider account and begin the verification process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your profile and organization">
    Add your practice name, address, specialty, accepted insurance plans, and office hours so patients can find and connect with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your EHR">
    Integrate your existing EHR system for automatic, bidirectional data sync. See the [EHR Integration guide](/providers/ehr-integration) for setup instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite patients to connect">
    Share your practice invite link or have patients search for you by name or NPI number in the MedifyAI app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Plans for providers

MedifyAI offers flexible pricing to fit practices of every size.

- **Free tier** — Designed for solo practitioners, the free tier supports up to 20 connected patients with core messaging and record-viewing features included.
- **Professional plan** — $49/month per provider. Includes unlimited patients, full bidirectional EHR sync, telehealth visits, and practice analytics.
- **Enterprise** — Custom pricing for health systems and multi-location organizations, including dedicated support, advanced analytics, and custom integrations. Contact [sales@medifyai.com](mailto:sales@medifyai.com) to discuss your needs.

<Note>
  All provider accounts are subject to identity and license verification before activation. This process typically takes 1–2 business days.
</Note>
