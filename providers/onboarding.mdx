This guide walks you through creating a MedifyAI provider account, completing credential verification, and configuring your practice profile so patients can find and connect with you.

## Provider onboarding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a provider account">
    Visit [app.medifyai.com/providers/signup](https://app.medifyai.com/providers/signup) and enter your name, email address, NPI number, and specialty to create your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your credentials">
    MedifyAI verifies your NPI and license against the NPPES registry automatically. You'll receive a confirmation email within 1–2 business days. In some cases, you may be asked to upload a copy of your medical license to complete verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your practice profile">
    Add your practice name, address, phone number, accepted insurance plans, and office hours. A complete profile helps patients find and connect with you more easily.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add other providers, nurses, or administrative staff under **Settings → Team**. Each team member receives their own login with configurable role-based permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your EHR">
    Integrate your existing EHR system for automatic data sync. Follow the instructions in the [EHR Integration guide](/providers/ehr-integration) to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite patients">
    Share your practice invite link or have patients search for you by name or NPI number directly in the MedifyAI app to begin connecting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Team permissions

MedifyAI uses role-based access control to ensure each team member only accesses what they need. The three available roles are:

- **Admin** — Full access to all practice settings, billing, team management, patient records, and integrations.
- **Provider** — Access to patient care features including health records, appointments, messaging, and prescription refills.
- **Staff** — Limited to scheduling and messaging only. Staff members cannot view clinical health records.

You can assign and update roles at any time from **Settings → Team**.

<Tip>
  Complete your practice profile fully — practices with a profile photo, bio, and accepted insurances receive significantly more patient connection requests.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Do not share your provider login credentials. Each team member should have their own account for audit and HIPAA compliance.
</Warning>
