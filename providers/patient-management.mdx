The MedifyAI provider dashboard gives you a consolidated view of your patients — their shared health records, upcoming appointments, active medications, and message threads — without leaving the platform.

## Your patient list

The patient list is your home base in the provider dashboard. From here you can:

- **Search** for a patient by name using the search bar at the top of the list.
- **Filter** patients by condition or last visit date to quickly surface the patients most relevant to your current workflow.
- **Sort** by upcoming appointment to prioritize who you need to see next.

Patients appear in your list after they connect with your practice through the MedifyAI app. You can also add patients manually via **Patients → Add Patient** if they have an existing MedifyAI account.

## Viewing patient health records

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a patient profile">
    Click a patient's name in your patient list to open their full profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Records tab">
    Select the **Records** tab to see all health records the patient has explicitly shared with your practice, including lab results, imaging, visit summaries, and uploaded documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View or download a record">
    Click any record to open it in the viewer. Use the **Download** button to save a copy as a PDF for your own records or referral purposes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Patient records are read-only from the provider view. Providers cannot modify or delete records that patients have uploaded.
</Note>

## Secure messaging

You can send and receive secure, HIPAA-compliant messages with patients directly from the **Messages** tab on any patient profile. Key details:

- **Attachments** — Send lab results, prescriptions, or other clinical documents as PDFs directly within a message thread.
- **Notifications** — Patients receive a push notification and email alert when you send them a message.
- **Thread history** — All messages are stored indefinitely and are auditable, supporting your HIPAA compliance obligations.

To start a new message thread, open a patient profile, select the **Messages** tab, and click **New Message**.

## Approving prescription refills

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Refill Requests">
    Go to **Refill Requests** using the bell icon in the top navigation bar or via the **Prescriptions** tab in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the request">
    Each refill request displays the patient's name, the requested medication, dosage, and the date it was last prescribed. Review this information before taking action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or deny the request">
    Click **Approve** to authorize the refill or **Deny** to decline it. If you deny a request, you can include a brief note explaining your decision. Once approved, the patient receives a confirmation and can pick up their medication from their preferred pharmacy.
  </Step>
</Steps>
