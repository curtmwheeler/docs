## Before you contact support

Taking a few minutes to gather information before reaching out will help us resolve your issue faster.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the troubleshooting guide">
    Review the [Common Issues](/troubleshooting/common-issues) page — many problems have quick self-service fixes that get you back up and running without waiting for a response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the MedifyAI status page">
    Visit [status.medifyai.com](https://status.medifyai.com) to see if there is an active incident or scheduled maintenance affecting the service. If there is, our team is already working on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note your details">
    Before contacting us, have the following ready — it significantly speeds up resolution:
    - Your **account email address**
    - The **device and browser** (or app version) you're using
    - A **clear description of the issue**, including any error messages you see
    - **Steps to reproduce** the problem, if applicable
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Response time expectations

| Channel | Expected Response Time |
|---|---|
| Live Chat | Same business day |
| Email | Within 24 hours |
| Billing disputes | 1–2 business days |
| Data deletion requests | Up to 30 days |

<Note>
