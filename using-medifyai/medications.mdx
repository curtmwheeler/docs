MedifyAI's medication tracker keeps all your prescriptions, over-the-counter medications, and supplements in one place. It sends you timely dose reminders, logs your adherence history, and automatically flags potential drug interactions — so you can stay on top of your health with confidence.

## Adding a medication

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Medications">
    Go to **Medications** in the navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new medication">
    Click **Add Medication** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the drug">
    Type the brand name or generic name (e.g., "Tylenol" or "acetaminophen"). Select the correct drug from the suggestions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter dosage details">
    Fill in the **dosage** (e.g., 500 mg), **frequency** (e.g., twice daily), and **start date**. Add an end date if the prescription is for a fixed duration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a reminder (optional)">
    Choose a daily reminder time to receive a notification when it is time to take your dose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The medication is added to your list and the interaction checker runs automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dose reminders

When a reminder time is set, MedifyAI notifies you via **push notification** and **email** at the scheduled time. From the notification you can:

- **Mark as Taken** — logs the dose as completed at that time.
- **Mark as Skipped** — records that you intentionally did not take the dose.
- **Snooze** — reminds you again in 30 minutes.

All taken and skipped doses are recorded in your **Adherence Calendar**, available on the medication's detail page. The calendar gives you and your provider a visual overview of your medication consistency over time.

## Drug interaction checker

Every time you add a new medication, MedifyAI automatically checks it against all other medications in your list. Interactions are flagged with one of three statuses:

- ✅ **Safe** — No known interaction. You can take these medications together as directed.
- ⚠️ **Caution** — A potential interaction exists. Monitor for specific symptoms listed in the alert and mention it to your provider at your next visit.
- 🚫 **Avoid** — A clinically significant interaction has been identified. Consult your doctor or pharmacist before taking both medications.

Interaction alerts appear immediately after saving a new medication and are also visible on the medication's detail page at any time.

<Warning>
  Always consult your **pharmacist or doctor** before making any changes to your medications, including stopping, starting, or adjusting doses. Do not rely solely on the interaction checker as a substitute for professional medical advice.
</Warning>

## Sharing your medication list

You can share your complete medication list with any healthcare provider at any time:

- Click **Share Medications** from the Medications page.
- Choose to generate a **PDF summary** (printable or downloadable) or send the list as a **secure message** to a connected provider.

This is especially useful before surgery, a new provider visit, or an emergency room visit where staff need a complete picture of what you are taking.

## Prescription refills

If your prescribing provider is connected to MedifyAI, you can request a prescription refill without calling the office:

1. Open the medication's detail page.
2. Click **Request Refill**.
3. Add an optional note to your provider (e.g., your current pill count or pharmacy preference).
4. Submit the request — your provider receives it for review and approval.

You'll receive a notification when the refill is approved and sent to your pharmacy.

<Tip>
  Set a consistent reminder time that aligns with your daily routine — for example, right after breakfast — to build a habit and improve your long-term medication adherence.
</Tip>
