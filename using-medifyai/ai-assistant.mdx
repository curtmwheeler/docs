The MedifyAI AI Assistant is an intelligent, conversational health companion available 24/7. Whether you want to understand a lab result, learn about a medication, or get guidance on managing a chronic condition, the Assistant is ready to help. This page explains how to start a conversation, what kinds of questions it can answer, and its limitations.

## Starting a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Assistant">
    Navigate to the Assistant from your dashboard or click the chat bubble icon in the bottom-right corner of any page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    Enter your question in plain, everyday language — no medical jargon required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the response">
    The AI returns a response that cites its sources and may ask follow-up questions to give you more accurate guidance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or share the conversation">
    Use the **Save** or **Share** options to keep a record of the conversation or send it directly to your healthcare provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can ask

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="General health questions">
    Ask anything you'd look up online — for example, "What are the symptoms of anemia?" or "How does ibuprofen work?" The Assistant draws on trusted medical sources to give you clear, evidence-based answers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Understanding your lab results">
    Paste or describe a lab result and ask what it means. For example: "My ferritin level is 8 ng/mL — is that low?" The Assistant explains what the value means, what the normal range is, and what follow-up questions to ask your doctor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Medication information">
    Ask about drug interactions, side effects, dosage guidance, and what to do if you miss a dose. For example: "Can I take melatonin with sertraline?"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Condition management tips">
    Get lifestyle advice for managing chronic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, or asthma — including diet, exercise, and monitoring recommendations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Finding a specialist">
    Not sure which type of doctor to see? Describe your concern and the Assistant will recommend the right specialty and help you book an appointment through MedifyAI.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Limitations

<Warning>
  The AI Assistant **cannot diagnose conditions, prescribe medications, or replace your doctor**. The information it provides is for educational purposes only. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call **911** or your local emergency number immediately.
</Warning>

## Conversation history

Every conversation you have with the AI Assistant is automatically saved to the **Messages** tab. You can:

- **Search** past conversations by keyword or date.
- **Share** a conversation with a connected provider directly from the Messages tab.
- **Delete** any conversation at any time from the conversation's settings menu — deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

<Tip>
  Be specific in your questions. Instead of "I feel bad," try "I have a dull headache behind my left eye that has lasted 3 days — what could be causing this?" More detail helps the Assistant give you a more accurate and useful response.
</Tip>
