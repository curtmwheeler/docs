The MedifyAI Symptom Checker guides you through a focused series of questions about how you feel and returns a ranked list of possible conditions along with clear, actionable next steps. It helps you make sense of your symptoms and decide whether to monitor, schedule an appointment, or seek urgent care.

<Warning>
  The Symptom Checker is for informational purposes only and **does not provide a medical diagnosis**. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for a proper evaluation and treatment plan.
</Warning>

## Running a symptom check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Symptom Checker">
    Click **Check Symptoms** from your dashboard or select it from the navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify the affected area">
    Select the part of the body affected using the interactive body diagram, or search directly by symptom name (e.g., "sore throat", "knee pain").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the follow-up questions">
    Provide additional details about your symptoms, including how long they have lasted, their severity on a scale of 1–10, and any other symptoms you are experiencing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your results">
    MedifyAI returns a list of possible conditions ranked from most to least likely, each with a likelihood badge and a brief description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your next step">
    Act on your results directly: book an appointment with a provider, continue the conversation in the AI Assistant, or dismiss the results if your symptoms have resolved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding your results

Each condition in your results includes a **likelihood badge** that reflects how closely your symptoms match that condition:

- **High** — Your symptoms are strongly associated with this condition. Consider contacting a provider soon.
- **Moderate** — Your symptoms are consistent with this condition, but other causes are equally possible. Monitor your symptoms and consult a provider if they worsen.
- **Low** — This condition is a less likely but possible explanation. It is included for completeness.

If any result is marked **Seek emergency care**, stop and get immediate medical attention — do not wait for an appointment.

Each result also includes a **Learn more** link that opens a detailed condition overview sourced from peer-reviewed medical references.

## When to call 911 immediately

<Warning>
  Do not use the Symptom Checker if you are experiencing any of the following — **call 911 or your local emergency number right away**:

  - Chest pain or pressure
  - Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
  - Sudden, severe headache unlike any you have had before
  - Signs of stroke — use the **FAST** test: **F**ace drooping, **A**rm weakness, **S**peech difficulty, **T**ime to call 911
  - Uncontrolled or heavy bleeding
  - Loss of consciousness or unresponsiveness
</Warning>

## Sharing results with your provider

After completing a symptom check, click the **Share** button on the results page to send a PDF summary to your healthcare provider. You can:

- Select a **connected provider** from your care team to send the summary via secure message.
- Enter a **provider's email address** to send the PDF as a secure, time-limited link.

Sharing your results before an appointment helps your provider prepare and makes the most of your time together.

<Tip>
  Run a symptom check before your next appointment to organize your thoughts and give your doctor a clear, structured picture of what you have been experiencing.
</Tip>
