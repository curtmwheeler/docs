MedifyAI lets you book, reschedule, and manage appointments with any of your connected healthcare providers without making a phone call. You can choose between in-person and telehealth visits, set reminders, and review appointment notes — all in one place.

<Note>
  Appointment availability depends on your connected providers. If you don't see any providers listed, ask your care team to connect their MedifyAI provider account, or search for a new provider using the **Find a Provider** tool.
</Note>

## Booking an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Appointments">
    Click **Appointments** in the navigation bar, then click **New Appointment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a provider">
    Search for a provider by name, specialty (e.g., "cardiologist"), or clinic name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an appointment type">
    Choose between **In-Person** or **Telehealth** based on your preference and the provider's availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date and time">
    Browse the provider's available slots and select the date and time that works best for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason for the visit">
    Enter a brief reason for the visit. This step is optional but helps your provider prepare and ensures you get the most out of your appointment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your booking">
    Click **Confirm**. You'll receive a confirmation email and a calendar invite (.ics) you can add to Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Telehealth appointments

Telehealth visits take place entirely inside MedifyAI — no third-party video app required. Here's what to expect:

- A **Join** button appears in your Appointments tab **15 minutes before** your scheduled start time.
- Click **Join** to enter the waiting room. Your provider will admit you when they are ready.
- Make sure you have a working **camera**, **microphone**, and a **stable internet connection** before joining.
- You can test your camera and microphone in advance via **Account → Device Settings**.

After the visit, any notes or follow-up instructions your provider adds are automatically stored in your **Health Records** under Visit Notes.

## Reminders

MedifyAI sends automatic reminders so you never miss an appointment:

- **24 hours before** — email and push notification.
- **1 hour before** — email and push notification.

You can customize which reminder channels you receive (email, push, or SMS) and adjust the timing in [Account → Notifications](/account/notifications).

## Rescheduling or cancelling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Appointments">
    Go to **Appointments** in the navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the appointment">
    Locate the upcoming appointment you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Click **Reschedule** to pick a new time slot, or click **Cancel** to cancel the appointment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a reason">
    Choose a reason from the dropdown. A reason is required for cancellations and helps your provider manage their schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your change">
    Click **Confirm**. You and your provider both receive an updated confirmation or cancellation notice by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Cancellations made within **2 hours** of the scheduled appointment time may be subject to the provider's cancellation or late-cancellation policy. Check the provider's policy before cancelling.
</Warning>
