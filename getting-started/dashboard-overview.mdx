Every key feature is accessible from here, designed so you can find what you need quickly and take action without navigating away. This page explains each section of the dashboard so you can get oriented fast.

## Top navigation bar

The navigation bar at the top of every page gives you direct access to all major areas of MedifyAI:

- **Home** — Return to your main dashboard at any time.
- **Health Records** — View and manage your full medical history, lab results, and documents.
- **Appointments** — Schedule new appointments, view upcoming visits, and review past ones.
- **Medications** — Manage your medication list, set reminders, and request refills.
- **Messages** — Communicate securely with your connected healthcare providers.
- **Settings** — Update your account details, notification preferences, privacy controls, and connected devices.

## Notifications

The **bell icon** in the top-right corner of the dashboard shows your unread notifications. MedifyAI sends notifications for upcoming appointments, medication reminders, new messages from providers, and important health alerts.

To manage what you're notified about and how, go to [Notification Preferences](/account/notifications). You can choose to receive notifications via in-app alerts, email, or push notifications on your mobile device.
