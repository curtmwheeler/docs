A complete health profile helps MedifyAI's AI give you more accurate, personalized guidance. This page walks you through adding your medical history, current medications, allergies, and emergency contacts — the key details that allow MedifyAI to tailor every recommendation to your specific needs.

## Your health profile

Your health profile is private by default. The information you add is never shared with third parties. It is only made available to healthcare providers you explicitly authorize and to MedifyAI's AI engine for the purpose of personalizing your health insights. You remain in full control of your data at all times.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile Settings">
    Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner of any page, then select **Profile** from the dropdown menu. This opens your health profile settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add basic health info">
    Fill in your foundational health details: **blood type**, **height**, **weight**, **biological sex**, and **date of birth**. These fields help the AI contextualize symptom checks and health recommendations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List current medications">
    Add each medication you currently take, including the **dosage** and **frequency** (e.g., 10 mg, once daily). Keeping this list accurate improves medication interaction checks and reminder scheduling. For more details, see the [Medications guide](/using-medifyai/medications).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add allergies and conditions">
    Enter any **known allergies** (e.g., penicillin, peanuts), **chronic conditions** (e.g., asthma, Type 2 diabetes), and relevant **past surgeries or procedures**. The AI uses this information to filter recommendations and flag potential risks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set emergency contacts">
    Add at least one emergency contact by entering their **full name** and **phone number**. This information is used only in emergency situations and is never shared otherwise.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Keep your profile up to date. Outdated information — such as old medications or an unrecorded diagnosis — may affect the accuracy of AI health recommendations.
</Warning>

## Profile visibility

Understanding who can see your health profile helps you stay in control of your data.

| Information | Visible to |
|---|---|
| Full health profile (all fields) | You only |
| Shared records & authorized data | Your care providers (only those you authorize) |
| Anonymized profile data | MedifyAI AI (used only for personalization) |

<Tip>
  Review and update your health profile at least every 6 months, or any time your medications, conditions, or emergency contacts change. Regular updates ensure the AI's guidance stays accurate and relevant.
</Tip>
