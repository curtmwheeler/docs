This page walks you through creating your MedifyAI account from start to finish. By the end, you'll have a verified account, a completed identity profile, and full access to your personal health dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
    Visit [app.medifyai.com/signup](https://app.medifyai.com/signup) in your browser. Enter your email address and create a strong password. Your password must be at least 8 characters long and include at least one number and one symbol (e.g., `!`, `@`, `#`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from MedifyAI. Click the verification link inside the email to confirm your address. The link is valid for 24 hours — if it expires, you can request a new one from the sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete identity verification">
    Enter your full legal name, date of birth, and country of residence. MedifyAI uses this information to personalize your health experience and to comply with applicable healthcare regulations. All data is encrypted and stored securely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept Terms & Privacy Policy">
    Review the MedifyAI Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then select **I agree** to continue. MedifyAI is fully HIPAA-compliant and your health data is encrypted both in transit and at rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your dashboard">
    You're all set. You'll be taken directly to your MedifyAI dashboard, where you can start setting up your health profile, exploring the AI assistant, and scheduling appointments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a personal email address rather than a work or school address when signing up. This ensures you always have access to your account and keeps your health information private.
</Tip>

## Sign in options

MedifyAI supports multiple sign-in methods so you can choose the option that works best for you:

- **Email & password** — Sign in with the email address and password you created during sign-up.
- **Google SSO** — Use your existing Google account to sign in with a single click.
- **Apple Sign In** — Sign in quickly and privately using your Apple ID.

<Note>
  If you ever lose access to your account, use the **Forgot password?** link on the sign-in page to reset your password via email. If you're still unable to access your account, contact MedifyAI Support for assistance with account recovery.
</Note>
